# Company Naming Strategy & Evaluation Sheet

This document compiles, evaluates, and ranks 20 premium brand names suitable for Karan Chordia’s AI strategy and content automation studio.

---

## 1. Naming Evaluation Methodology

Each name is scored on a scale of 1 to 5 (with 5 being the highest) across five criteria:
1.  **Sanskrit/Indian Resonance (IR)**: Depth of cultural meaning and local familiarity.
2.  **International Usability (IU)**: Ease of pronunciation, spellcheck ease, and global appeal.
3.  **Premium/B2B Feel (PF)**: Professional authority and suitability for high-ticket enterprise buyers.
4.  **Memorability (MB)**: Brand recall and phonetic strength.
5.  **Domain Risk (DR)**: Estimated availability of premium TLDs (like `.ai`, `.com`, `.studio`) without exorbitant costs (5 = lowest risk, 1 = highest risk).

## 3. Shortlisted Top 5 & Recommendations

### 1. Sutra Automations / SutraLogic (`[Recommendation]`)
*   *Tone:* Authoritative, precise, enterprise-ready.
*   *Why it wins:* "Sutra" translates to a thread or rule. In computer science, this perfectly maps to routing workflows, sequences, and logic nodes. It is highly clean, easy for international clients to pronounce, and sounds premium.
*   *Domain Status:* The `.ai` or `.studio` suffixes (e.g., `sutraautomations.ai`, `sutralogic.ai`) are highly likely to be available at standard registration fees (`[Inference]`).

### 2. Vyom Dynamics
*   *Tone:* Expansive, technological, modern.
*   *Why it’s strong:* "Vyom" means space or sky. It serves as a subtle nod to Karan’s drone mapping and spatial videography history, while "Dynamics" positions the firm alongside elite consulting engineering brands.
*   *Domain Status:* Highly likely to have cheap domain registration options (`[Inference]`).

### 3. Katha Automata
*   *Tone:* Creative, technical, storytelling-centric.
*   *Why it’s strong:* By pairing the Sanskrit word for story ("Katha") with the computer science term "Automata", it perfectly captures Karan’s unique differentiator: automated narrative engines.
*   *Domain Status:* Excellent availability potential (`[Inference]`).

### 4. Tattva.ai
*   *Tone:* Minimalist, elite, philosophical.
*   *Why it’s strong:* "Tattva" represents elements or fundamental principles. It represents distilling the complex noise of generative AI into actionable corporate value.
*   *Domain Status:* Needs verification, but `tattva.ai` might require a premium purchase.

### 5. Bodhi Flux
*   *Tone:* Modern, transformative, insightful.
*   *Why it’s strong:* Merging "Bodhi" (enlightenment) with "Flux" (flow/continuous change) frames the consultancy as a guide leading enterprises through the rapid AI shift.
*   *Domain Status:* Very low competition (`[Inference]`).
