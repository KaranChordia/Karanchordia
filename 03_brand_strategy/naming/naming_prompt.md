# Naming Generator Prompt

Copy and paste the prompt below into Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT to generate new brand name variations.

```text
Act as an elite brand naming strategist and creative director. I need help naming my new B2B AI strategy and content automation studio. 

Here is the context about me and my business:

1. MY BACKGROUND:
- I am a Bengaluru-based AI Architect and Creator.
- I spent years in commercial videography, drone cinematography, and B2B media (e.g., directing vlogs and inaugurals for WeWork India and Hyatt Centric).
- I pivoted into complex algorithmic systems, spending years in high-frequency crypto trading (mastering risk management and quantitative systems logic).
- I transitioned into generative AI, building custom GPTs, contributing to technical OpenAI developer forums, and developing internal automation tools/marketing content for a workforce consulting corporate client (Nexocean).

2. THE BUSINESS DUALITY (MY DIFFERENTIATOR):
- Many AI consultants are pure engineers (their content/system outputs are creatively dry and sterile).
- Many creative agencies lack technical rigor (they don't know how to deploy API workflows, code custom agents, or manage JSON/databases).
- My studio sits at the intersection: I bring narrative empathy (storytelling, visual style) and computational logic (APIs, custom AI agents, automated repurposing workflows).

3. SERVICES I OFFER:
- AI Workflow Audits & Strategy Blueprints (entry sprits).
- Founder Narrative Kits (storytelling videos + AI-powered content distribution bundles).
- Autonomous Agent Architecture (building custom API workflows and database-integrated AI agents).
- AI-Powered Content Engines (recurring monthly strategy and automation retainers).

4. MY ICP (TARGET CLIENTS):
- Tech founders, Seed to Series-B B2B SaaS startups, prop-tech platforms, and modern workspace/co-working networks. They expect premium, authoritative, and clean services.

5. WHAT I WANT IN A NAME:
- It should project authority, premium B2B consulting feel, and clean international usability.
- I like the structure of "Narrative Logic" (which directly bridges storytelling/expression and system logic).
- I want suggestions that are either:
  a) Clean international names (not necessarily Indian).
  b) Hybrid/Indian-infused names that blend Sanskrit/Indian linguistic root terms representing speech/voice/story (e.g., Katha, Vak/Vaac) with strategic logic/system rules (e.g., Yukti, Sutra, Tantra).
- Names I already like:
  - Loom Logic (weaving media threads + backend automation)
  - Narrative Logic (storytelling + systems engineering)
  - Yukti Narrative (Yukti = logic/reasoning/strategy)
  - Katha Logic (Katha = story/tale)
  - Vaac Logic (Vaac = speech/voice)
  
6. WHAT TO AVOID:
- Avoid cheap-sounding or generic "AI wrapper" names (e.g., "AI Smart Solutions", "Karan Consulting", "SuperAI", "PromptMaster").
- Avoid overly complex Sanskrit terms that international clients cannot pronounce or spell easily.

Please generate a categorized list of 20 unique, premium, and professional name ideas. For each name, explain:
1. The meaning/rationale behind the combination.
2. The brand tone it projects.
3. Suggest TLD extensions (e.g., .ai, .studio, .com).
```
