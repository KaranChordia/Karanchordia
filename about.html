02 / Working principles

What stays
in the room.

A

Attention before assumption.

Look closely enough to understand the real shape of the work—not merely the category it arrived in.

B

Structure before automation.

Name the workflow, the handoff, and the human checkpoint before asking technology to accelerate it.

C

Clarity before theatre.

Make the value legible. If the story requires fog to sound impressive, the system probably needs more work.
