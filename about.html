✳

A maker’s path.
My own way into it.

I’m Karan Chordia, an independent maker and storyteller based in Bengaluru, and the founder of Kramaniti.

I didn’t come through a conventional developer path. I came through curiosity: trying tools, asking questions, and learning by making. That habit now runs through the websites, digital products, tools, and content I create.

I’m interested in the whole experience: how an idea is presented, how the work behind it happens, and how its story reaches people.

A few chapters

01 / A camera and a question

I wanted to explain technology.
I found a love for creating.

I began freelancing in video with the idea of explaining tech. Filming, editing, and finding the story quickly became interests in their own right. HowDramaTech became a place to explore gadgets, places, and everyday life through a camera.

02 / From personal to commissioned

One film led
to another.

I made videos at WeWork locations in Bengaluru. That led to an official Pride Week project with WeWork and, over time, more brand films through word of mouth. I learned to take an idea from a conversation to something an audience could see and feel.

03 / A different set of tools

The curiosity
kept going.

Alongside content, I kept tinkering with technology and exploring AI. At Nexocean, from September 2025 to March 2026, I made recruiting tools and content, bringing both interests into the same work.

04 / Bringing it together

Technology, stories,
and Kramaniti.

I founded Kramaniti to bring these threads together. It’s where I explore how a brand’s presence, its everyday tools, and its outward storytelling can support one another.
