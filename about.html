About Karan

I help make
complicated work
easier to understand.

I’m Karan Chordia, founder of Kramaniti. I work with practical AI, workflows, and communication—based in Bengaluru.

I began in visual storytelling and creative production. That taught me to notice what people need to see and understand. Today I apply the same thinking to how teams work, make decisions, and explain what they do.

02 / How I tend to work

A few simple
rules I come back to.

A

Understand it before changing it.

First look at what is really happening, instead of assuming the label tells the whole story.

B

Get the process clear before automating it.

Agree on the workflow, the handoff, and who checks the work before asking technology to speed it up.

C

Say it plainly.

If an idea needs too much fog to sound impressive, it probably needs more work—not bigger words.
