About Karan

I help teams make
work easier to run
and easier to explain.

I’m Karan Chordia, founder of Kramaniti. I work across practical AI, workflow design, documentation, and communication from Bengaluru.

My starting point is simple: understand how the work moves today—its decisions, handoffs, bottlenecks, and places where people need to stay involved—before changing it.

02 / How I work

A useful system
still needs good judgment.

A

See the work before changing it.

Look at what is actually happening, including the workarounds and decisions a diagram does not show.

B

Get clear before you automate.

Agree on the workflow, handoff, and review point before asking technology to make it faster.

C

Make the explanation usable.

A good process needs clear documentation and communication, not more language for people to decode.
