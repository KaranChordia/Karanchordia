About me

I’ve always been
the person
friends call
about tech.

I’m Karan Chordia, founder of Kramaniti, based in Bengaluru. I started out telling stories with a camera, learned how people find a message and act on it, and spent years directing content for brands.

Through all of it I never stopped tinkering: trying new tools, changing settings, making technology fit the person in front of it. Today those two habits are my work.

01 / My story

One thread,
a few chapters.

  1. 2016 to 2017

    Digital Marketing

    At Web Marketing Academy I learned how people come across a message, make sense of it, and decide what to do next.

  2. 2016 to 2017

    Photo & Videography

    Freelance visual work taught me to notice the small details that hold attention.

  3. Aug 2017 to Dec 2022

    Content Director

    I took stories from first idea to finished film and out into the world, always keeping the audience in mind.

  4. Mar 2023 to 2025

    AI Architect

    At Aimpact Space I moved deeper into technology, building with AI and seeing where it could make real work easier.

  5. May 2026 to now

    Founder, Kramaniti

    I now help teams and business owners get comfortable with technology and explain their work clearly.
