Hey, I’m Karan. I help people make friends with the technology that could change their work.

Curious about tools. Clear about people.

For about ten years I’ve loved two things: telling stories and tinkering with technology until it fits the way people actually work. Now I bring them together. I sit with business owners and teams who know tech could help them, but feel it isn’t really for them, and we figure it out side by side.
